SC ON THE PICOCALC: YOUR FIRST SHEET
====================================
Ten minutes, start to finish: a small monthly budget with a total,
percentages, money formatting, a row added in the middle, and a
saved file. SC.TXT has the reference; this is the walk-through.

Every step was tried exactly as written. Keys are in capitals only
where the capital matters: sc treats f and F, p and P as different
commands.


1. START
Pick sc from the menu. You see an empty grid: columns A, B, C...
across the top, rows 0, 1, 2... down the side (sc counts rows from
0). The highlighted cell is A0. The top line tells you which cell
you're on and what's in it.


2. TYPE THE LABELS
Text goes in with < (left-aligned) and Enter.
  <Rent  Enter        then Down arrow
  <Food  Enter        Down
  <Car   Enter        Down
  <Fun   Enter        Down, Down (leave row 4 empty)
  <Total Enter

You now have five labels in column A, rows 0 to 5.


3. TYPE THE NUMBERS
Numbers and formulas go in with =. Move to B0 (Right arrow, then
Up until the top line says B0) and type:
  =1200  Enter        Down
  =450   Enter        Down
  =300   Enter        Down
  =120   Enter        Down, Down

On B5, next to Total:
  =@sum(B0:B3)  Enter

B5 shows 2070.00. Change any number (move to it, =, new value,
Enter) and the total follows at once.


4. PERCENTAGES, AND COPYING A FORMULA
Move to C0 and type
  =B0/B$5  Enter
That's this row's amount over the total. The $ in B$5 means "always
row 5": when the formula is copied, B0 will become B1, B2 and so on,
but B$5 stays put.

Now copy it down. With the cursor still on C0:
  m a        marks C0 as "a" (m, then the letter a)
  Down, c a  copies mark a into C1
  Down, c a  C2
  Down, c a  C3
  Down, Down, c a   C5

Column C shows 0.58, 0.22, 0.14, 0.06 and, next to Total, 1.00.


5. MAKE IT LOOK RIGHT
f sets the format of the whole column you're on: f, Space, then
width, decimals and a format number, then Enter.
  On column C:  f  Space  8 1 9  Enter     shows 58.0%, 21.7%...
  On column B:  f  Space  11 2 8 Enter     shows $1,200.00...

Format 9 (percent) and 8 (money) come from scrc.txt in /sc; SC.TXT,
FORMATS, lists the others, including dates.


6. ADD A ROW IN THE MIDDLE
Forgot the phone bill? Move to the Car row and type
  i r        insert a row above the cursor
Then put the new line in: Left to column A, <Phone Enter, Right,
=65 Enter. The total becomes 2135.00 by itself: sc stretched
@sum(B0:B3) to @sum(B0:B4) when the row went in. Copy the percent
formula into the new row's C cell with c a, as before.

d r deletes the row you're on, and the total shrinks to match.


7. SAVE IT, CLOSE IT, OPEN IT
  P budget.sc  Enter     saves (it goes in /sc on the card)
  q                      back to the menu
Start sc again, then
  G budget.sc  Enter     opens it, formulas and all

To share it with a PC, also save a copy as CSV:
  P budget.csv  Enter
A .csv keeps the numbers you see but not the formulas, so keep the
.sc file as your working copy.


8. WHEN YOU'RE STUCK
  Esc          backs out one step; twice cancels a line you're typing
  Ctrl+g       cancels whatever you're in the middle of
  ?            help, 16 pages (a letter picks one; Esc leaves)
  i> / e>      the start of the top line: i> means you're typing,
               e> means letters are commands (Esc gets you there;
               i or A gets you back to typing)

There is no undo. Save before big changes (P, same name, Enter).


9. WHERE NEXT
- The four sample sheets in /sc: budget.sc, loan.sc (a loan with a
  payment table), dates.sc and convert.sc. Open each with G and
  look at the formulas on the top line as you move around.
- Help page K lists everything you can put in a formula; pages L
  to P list the functions (@sum, @avg, @pmt, @date and the rest).
- Sheets can be written on a PC in Notepad; SC.TXT, BUILDING
  SHEETS ON THE PC, shows how.
